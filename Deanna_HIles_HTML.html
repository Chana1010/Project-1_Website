Sedgwick Ave Display

Desciption

Sedgwick Ave Display is a free, graffiti-inspired Google Font designed by Pedro Vergani and Kevin Burke that captures the energetic urban aesthetic of the South Bronx birthplace of hip hop for large-scale headlines.

Finding fonts that evoked sushi was a challenge, as I needed something akin to Japanese typefaces which are typically delicate and refined. However, after examining sushi menus and related materials, I realized that *Sedgwick Ace Display* was a perfect match for what I was seeking. Inspired by graffiti and created by Pedro Vergani and Kevin Burke, the font captures the urban style of the South Bronx the birthplace of hip-hop and is designed for large-scale headlines.

To me, however, the font evokes sushi thanks to its elongated, stretched style. Its graffiti-inspired aesthetic feels playful and approachable rather than stiff, yet it remains formal enough to work effectively on a sushi menu. In practice, using *Sedgwick Ace Display* for main headings helps guide the diner's eye fluidly across the menu's various sections. Its dynamic stroke breaks the monotony of more traditional typefaces, adding freshness and personality without sacrificing the clarity needed to read the dish names.
